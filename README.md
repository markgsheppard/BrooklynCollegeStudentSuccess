# Brooklyn College Economics: Student Success & Graduate Pathways

A design prototype for a Brooklyn College Department of Economics page that brings together internships, graduate school preparation (M.S. in Economic Analysis and the CUNY Graduate Center Ph.D.), faculty research, seminars, publishing, funding and mentorship.

- `index.html`: the main Student Success page
- `opportunities.html`: the full, filterable list of internships, research assistant jobs and programs (last updated November 10, 2026)
- `dc-lite.js`: a small renderer for the pages' templates and filters

This is a proposal, not an official brooklyn.edu page. Confirm all dates on the official postings.
